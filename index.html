Where these numbers come from. Plan features, per-user rates and user minimums are transcribed from the WorkflowMax by BlueRock pricing page (workflowmax.com/pricing), read on 18 September 2026. WorkflowMax publishes its own rate card in NZD, AUD, USD, GBP and CAD — those figures are used exactly as published, not converted. Euro is not a currency WorkflowMax bills in; the EUR option is a conversion from NZD at the editable mid-market rate shown, and is indicative only.

Assumptions worth checking before you quote. Quarterly and annual figures are the published per-user-per-month rate for that cadence multiplied by 3 and by 12 respectively. NZ prices are exclusive of GST (15%); in other jurisdictions, check whether local sales tax, VAT or GST is added on top. Essentials is sold for 3–25 users, Pro from 5 users and Advanced from 10 users, so a team below a plan's minimum is billed at the minimum. Dedicated account manager, guided onboarding and sandbox access are listed as requiring a minimum of 25 users on an annual subscription.
